01 · Nextdoor Paid Media

A local campaign built from scratch.

The campaign moved from a generic service message to a defined AC-replacement offer. I coordinated the three placements, equipment-led creative, Get Quote CTA, and dedicated GoHighLevel destination.

Scope
  • Reframed the offer around AC replacement from $89/month
  • Moved from a call-first path to a quote-form funnel
  • Prepared Newsfeed, Conversations, and marketplace placements
  • Set a controlled $75/day, 14-day test
  • Reconciled platform, form, workflow, and CRM results
$1,050Total test spend
102,458Impressions
662Clicks
$1.59Cost per click
Decision

The campaign produced visibility and traffic but no quote-form submissions. I recommended pausing another paid run and retaining the established organic Nextdoor presence instead of repeating the spend without a stronger lead signal.

02 · PPC & LSA Analytics

Platform metrics translated into business results.

Google Ads and Local Services Ads were reviewed against ServiceTitan calls, booked jobs, completed jobs, and revenue. The audit separated soft conversions from outcomes that leadership could actually use.

Audit method
  • Collected activity from first launch through YTD reporting
  • Aligned spend and lead windows by month
  • Reconciled calls and jobs inside ServiceTitan
  • Separated confirmed from possibly influenced revenue
  • Calculated CPL, cost per job, and ROAS

03 · Attribution & Reporting

Connecting spend, calls, jobs, and revenue.

Useful reporting depends on every handoff between the ad, phone number, CRM campaign, booking record, job status, and invoice. I audited that chain and identified where source attribution could be lost.

System controls
  • Dedicated campaign-number mapping
  • Paid, organic, and Local Services source separation
  • Monthly campaign-cost entry process
  • CSR campaign-handling rules
  • Follow-up attribution window
  • Manual-call and unmatched-lead review

Result

The process distinguished confirmed campaign revenue from broad phone matches that could contain repeat customers or follow-up work, producing a more conservative and defensible report.
