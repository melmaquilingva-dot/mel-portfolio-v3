Case Study 01 · Local Paid Media

Building and auditing a Nextdoor campaign from scratch.

The campaign moved from a generic service message to a defined AC-replacement offer. I coordinated the campaign structure, three Nextdoor placements, equipment-led creative, Get Quote CTA, and dedicated HighLevel landing page.

Scope of work
  • Reframed the offer around AC replacement from $89/month
  • Replaced a call-first path with a quote-form funnel
  • Prepared Newsfeed, Conversations, and marketplace placements
  • Set a controlled $75/day, 14-day test
  • Reviewed traffic, form records, CRM enrollments, and final outcome
$1,050Total test spend
102,458Impressions
662Clicks
$1.59Cost per click
Decision

The campaign produced visibility and traffic but no quote-form submissions. I recommended pausing another paid run, retaining the established organic Nextdoor presence, and avoiding another $1,050 commitment without a stronger lead signal. The value of the work was the decision quality as much as the launch itself.

Case Study 02 · PPC & LSA Analytics

Turning ad-platform metrics into revenue-based reporting.

I reviewed Google Ads and Local Services Ads against ServiceTitan calls, booked jobs, completed jobs, and revenue. The audit separated soft platform conversions from business outcomes and kept uncertain attribution out of the confirmed totals.

Audit method
  • Collected performance from campaign launch through YTD reporting
  • Matched monthly spend and lead windows across platforms
  • Reconciled calls and jobs inside ServiceTitan
  • Separated confirmed revenue from possible influenced revenue
  • Converted platform reporting into CPL, cost per job, and ROAS

Finding

Google’s low displayed cost per conversion counted softer actions. The operating view showed approximately $622 per booked job and $670 per completed job. This changed the discussion from “How many conversions did Google report?” to “What did each booked and completed job cost?”

Case Study 03 · Attribution & Reporting

Connecting ad spend, calls, jobs, and revenue.

A useful report depends on clean handoffs between the ad platform, phone number, CRM campaign, booking record, job status, and invoice. I audited that chain and documented where source attribution could be lost or manually changed.

System controls
  • Dedicated campaign-number mapping
  • Paid LSA, paid search, and organic source separation
  • Monthly campaign-cost entry process
  • Campaign attribution and CSR handling rules
  • Follow-up job attribution window
  • Manual-call and unmatched-lead review

Result

The reporting process distinguished confirmed campaign revenue from broader phone-number matches that could include repeat customers or follow-up work. Leadership received a more conservative and defensible performance view.
